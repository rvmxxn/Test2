Barr Body Analysis[span_79](end_span)
[span_80](start_span)

A Barr body (sex chromatin body) is the inactivated, condensed X chromosome visible in somatic cells[span_80](end_span).

    [span_81](start_span)
  • Based on the Lyon Hypothesis: Inactivation is random and occurs early in embryonic development[span_81](end_span).
    • [span_82](start_span)
  • Formula: Number of Barr bodies = Number of X chromosomes − 1[span_82](end_span).
    • [span_83](start_span)
  • This Patient: 45,X → 1 X chromosome − 1 = 0 Barr Bodies (Absent)[span_83](end_span).
[span_84](start_span)Clinical Utility[span_84](end_span) [span_85](start_span)Buccal mucosal scraping sent for analysis confirms the absence of a Barr body, confirming the 45,X karyotype[span_85](end_span). [span_86](start_span)It is a quick, cheap screening test in resource-limited settings[span_86](end_span).

High-Yield Exam Preparation[span_107](end_span)[span_108](end_span)

What is the diagnosis in this case? [span_109](start_span)Give reasons.[span_109](end_span)

Turner Syndrome (45,X). [span_110](start_span)Key evidence: Primary amenorrhea, short stature, webbed neck, shield chest, cubitus valgus, streak ovaries on USS, very high FSH & LH (hypergonadotropic hypogonadism), very low estradiol, absent Barr body on buccal smear.[span_110](end_span)

[span_111](start_span)Why are FSH and LH elevated in this patient?[span_111](end_span)

Streak ovaries produce no estrogen. Estrogen normally exerts negative feedback on the hypothalamus and anterior pituitary. [span_112](start_span)Without estrogen, there is no negative feedback → GnRH keeps being released → FSH and LH remain elevated (hypergonadotropic state).[span_112](end_span)

[span_113](start_span)What is a Barr body and what is its significance here?[span_113](end_span)

A Barr body is the inactivated X chromosome (heterochromatin) visible at the periphery of the nucleus. Number of Barr bodies = Number of X chromosomes − 1. In Turner syndrome (45,X): only one X → no inactivation needed → 0 Barr bodies. [span_114](start_span)Absent Barr body confirms 45,X karyotype.[span_114](end_span)

[span_115](start_span)Why is the uterus present if she has no functional ovaries?[span_115](end_span)

The uterus is derived from Müllerian ducts. Müllerian ducts regress only in the presence of Müllerian Inhibiting Factor (MIF/AMH) secreted by fetal testes. [span_116](start_span)In Turner syndrome, there are no functional testes → no MIF → Müllerian ducts develop normally → uterus and fallopian tubes are present.[span_116](end_span)

[span_117](start_span)[span_118](start_span)What is the rationale for prescribing Ethinyl Estradiol and Progesterone?[span_117](end_span)[span_118](end_span)

Ethinyl Estradiol replaces absent ovarian estrogen. [span_119](start_span)It induces secondary sexual characteristics, stimulates endometrial proliferation, and protects bone density[span_119](end_span). Unopposed estrogen causes endometrial hyperplasia, which can progress to cancer. [span_120](start_span)Progesterone is added sequentially to shed the endometrium (withdrawal bleed), protecting against this risk[span_120](end_span).

[span_121](start_span)What is the SHOX gene and its role in Turner syndrome?[span_121](end_span)

SHOX (Short Stature Homeobox) gene is located on the pseudoautosomal region of the X chromosome. It regulates skeletal growth. [span_122](start_span)In Turner syndrome (only one X), SHOX haploinsufficiency causes short stature and skeletal anomalies (cubitus valgus, Madelung deformity).[span_122](end_span)
